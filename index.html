Common Questions
How long does one video take?

Standard turnaround time is 12 to 36 hours per short-form video, depending on complexity and motion graphics requirements.

Do you provide the B-roll and music?

Yes! I have access to premium stock footage libraries, copyright-free trending music, and high-impact sound effects (SFX) to elevate your raw footage.

What if I need a revision?

Client satisfaction is my top priority. I include fast, collaborative revisions until you are 100% happy with the pacing, captions, and final look.

Do you offer ongoing monthly packages or bulk discounts?

Yes! Most of my agency and creator clients work with me on weekly or monthly retainer batches (15–30+ videos/month) with dedicated priority turnaround.

What software do you use for editing?

I edit natively in Adobe Premiere Pro and create custom animations, UI callouts, and visual hooks in Adobe After Effects.
